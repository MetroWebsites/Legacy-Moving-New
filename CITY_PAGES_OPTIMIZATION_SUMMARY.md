## SEO Optimization Strategy

### Search Intent Targeting
Each page is optimized for the most common search queries:
- ✅ "moving company in [City]"
- ✅ "movers in [City]"
- ✅ "[City] moving company"
- ✅ "[City] movers"
- ✅ "professional movers [City]"

### Unique Content Differentiation

Every city page has:
1. **Unique H1** - No duplicate titles across any city
2. **Unique intro paragraph** - Different messaging, keywords, and selling points
3. **City-specific benefits** - Each page highlights what makes that city unique:
   - Denver → Trust & reviews
   - Aurora → Affordability & transparency
   - Lakewood → Local + storage
   - Arvada → Speed & reliability
   - Westminster → Same-day availability
   - Thornton → Large home expertise
   - Centennial → Premium & corporate
   - Highlands Ranch → White-glove service
   - Littleton → Value & customer service
   - Englewood → Quick quotes
   - Golden → Mountain/foothills expertise
   - Boulder → Student & flexible scheduling

### Content Writing Approach
- **Natural & Human**: No keyword stuffing, smooth conversational tone
- **Search-Focused**: Incorporates actual search terms people use
- **Action-Oriented**: Clear CTAs in every intro (call, quote, schedule)
- **Trust Signals**: Licensed, insured, reviews, experience mentioned strategically
